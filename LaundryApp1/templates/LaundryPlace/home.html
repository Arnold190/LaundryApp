About Us

We are a dry cleaning and laundry company that provides a total service package for personal and corporate dry cleaning and laundry solutions.

Why Choose Us

Best Pricing in Kampala

We offer the most competitive prices in Kampala without compromising on quality. Our goal is to provide top-notch laundry services that are affordable for everyone. By choosing us, you get the best value for your money, ensuring your clothes are impeccably clean without breaking the bank.

Convenience

We understand that your time is precious. That's why we offer a range of convenient services tailored to fit your busy lifestyle. From easy online booking to pick-up and delivery options, we make the process of getting your laundry done as hassle-free as possible. You can focus on what matters most while we take care of the rest.

Quality Cleaning

Quality is at the heart of our service. Our team of experienced professionals uses state-of-the-art equipment and high-quality detergents to ensure your garments are treated with the utmost care. Whether it's everyday clothing or delicate fabrics, we guarantee a thorough clean that will leave your clothes looking and feeling their best.

How We Work

Our Users Sign-Up on our page, so that they are integrated into our system. They place a Call to have their specifics taken or added. Normal clients can also proceed to just placing a call. But this gives them No-Discount Offers, as they are not in our system!

Orders are then entered by the PoS (Point of Sale) Aministrator/personnel. With status of the Orders recorded and tracked. We call our Clients and inform them when their Orders are ready.

Delivery can also be arranged!
